Future Health Digital Innovation Challenge 2026 · Wellbeing track

To sense is to predict — on your own device.

WellSense helps people notice changes in their wellbeing early, keep healthier habits, and get personal support over time — with an AI that runs entirely on their own computer, so their health data never leaves it.

1 · Sense

One-minute daily check-in: mood, energy, stress, sleep, water, activity and optional blood pressure. A weekly two-question mood and anxiety check.

→

2 · Predict

WellSense learns each person's own baseline and flags early drifts — less sleep, rising stress, falling mood or activity — days before they add up.

→

3 · Support

A private AI coach turns those signals into small, practical next steps, and a safety layer points to emergency help and crisis lines when it matters.

Evaluation on simulated users

Why it matters

  • Earlier, not later. Personal baselines spot change sooner than one-size-fits-all thresholds.
  • Private by design. All data and AI stay on the device. No account, no cloud, no upload.
  • Works offline and on modest hardware. Tested on a 2018 laptop processor with no special AI chip.
  • Explainable. Every signal says what changed, by how much, and compared with what.
  • Made for the region. English and Arabic, with Pakistan and UAE emergency numbers and support lines.
  • Safe. Red-flag words in English or Arabic trigger emergency guidance and local crisis numbers immediately.

How it's built

Limits

WellSense gives general wellbeing information and pattern alerts. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose. Screening questions (PHQ-2, GAD-2) suggest when to talk to a professional; they don't replace one. The Arabic screening wording is our translation, not the officially validated Arabic version. In an emergency call your local ambulance number.
